# Neutral traffic vehicles

Only sedan and SUV are used. These are separate modern source models, with neutral gray materials and opaque windows. Brand emblems, the sedan's recessed grille assembly, exhaust and interior details are removed or covered by continuous front surfaces. A separate opaque, closed body core prevents panel seams exposing an empty cabin. Alpha remains opaque; the exterior paint uses only 4% physical transmission, a broad weak highlight and a 10% restrained cool-blue admixture.

Sedan: [Audi A7 Sportback 2018](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/95700e5a98904b3591c6a03a4b67787b), by [KiW](https://sketchfab.com/QlER5), CC BY 4.0. Source exterior triangles retained for adaptation: 225,575; prepared asset: 27,789 triangles, including the sealed core. The clean wheel geometry is adapted from the credited SUV source. The source grille/emblems are removed and a closed neutral fascia replaces them.

SUV: [2015 Hyundai Tucson](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/7674a3bd168a451daf73fc0a33d39589), by [razor24](https://sketchfab.com/razor24), CC BY 4.0. Source exterior triangles retained for adaptation: 25,712; prepared asset: 21,732 triangles, including the sealed core. The branded grille is flattened under a continuous curved front surface.
