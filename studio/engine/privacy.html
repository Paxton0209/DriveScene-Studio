Privacy

Updated 14 October 2026. DriveScene Studio runs in your browser. You do not need an account, and exported images are generated locally.

Usage measurement

The hosted site uses Google Analytics 4 to help its creator understand visits, referral websites and PNG exports. Reports are private to the site owner. Analytics is off until you choose Allow. Before consent, no Google Analytics script or measurement request is loaded. If you allow analytics, Google uses first-party analytics cookies with a 30-day lifetime to distinguish browsers and sessions, and receives connection information such as your IP address and browser information. We keep advertising storage and personalization denied, and disable Google signals. Counts cover consenting browsers only; they are not an exact count of distinct people.

Our explicit events are a page view when the site loads and png_export after an image is generated and its download is initiated. Export events include the scene category, light/dark theme and image dimensions. They do not confirm that a file was saved to disk. We remove query parameters and fragments from the reported page URL, and limit the reported referrer to its origin. We do not send image contents, typed text, email addresses or account details. Google may also process standard engagement signals as described in its documentation.

See how Google uses information from sites that use its services and Google's consent-mode documentation. Requests may be blocked by your browser or network. The site and PNG export continue working when analytics is unavailable.

Your choice

We skip analytics when your browser sends Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control. You can also withdraw consent for this browser using the button below. This stores only your opt-out preference locally. Closing any already-open DriveScene Studio tabs and reopening the site ensures that a previously loaded Google script is no longer active.

Local preferences and hosting

The site stores your theme choice locally. GitHub Pages hosts the website and may process connection logs under GitHub's privacy statement. For questions, contact the creator through the project repository.
